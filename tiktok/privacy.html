Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Market Brief Studio ("the App") is a personal tool that posts the developer's own videos to the developer's own TikTok account.

Data we access

Through TikTok Login Kit the App receives an OAuth access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) for the authorizing account, solely to upload and publish videos to that account and confirm publish status.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the developer's own computer, are never shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party, and are used for no other purpose.

Third parties

No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); local tokens are deleted on revocation or on request.
